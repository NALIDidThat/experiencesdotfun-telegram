---
name: Journey learning links
description: Privacy and completion boundaries for learning resources in shared programmes.
---

Organisation programme resource steps must not expose participant-owned saved research or flashcard decks. Keep shared learning content as links unless the product adds an explicit sharing model. Resource steps are informational and do not count as completed lessons or award XP until real lesson completion criteria exist.

**Why:** Research and decks are profile-scoped, while programmes are authored for multiple participants. Treating a saved/opened resource as learning completion would also give credit without evidence.

**How to apply:** Preserve private learning ownership when extending programme resources; only add completion credit after defining and verifying lesson-level evidence.
