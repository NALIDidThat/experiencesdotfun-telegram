---
name: AI assistant product actions
description: Privacy, role, and publishing constraints for Experiences.fun assistant actions across the Mini App and Telegram.
---

Natural-language assistant actions should use the same Experiences.fun tools in the app and Telegram. Personal journey data is available only in a private, unambiguous Participant profile context; group chats may use public discovery and research only. Hosted experience history and draft preparation require an Organisation profile. Drafts must open the existing creation flow for user review and Maps location confirmation; the assistant never publishes or joins on the user's behalf.

**Why:** Experiences.fun needs a natural entry point into its real participation loop without weakening profile privacy or existing publishing permissions.

**How to apply:** Reuse shared assistant tools across channels, enforce roles on the server, and keep consequential participation or publishing actions explicitly user-confirmed.
