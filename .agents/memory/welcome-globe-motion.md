---
name: Welcome globe motion
description: The welcome screen's explicit continuous globe-animation requirement.
---

The Experiences.fun welcome globe should continue rotating even when the operating system has reduced motion enabled. Other globe views should continue respecting reduced-motion preferences.

**Why:** the user explicitly asked for the welcome globe to spin constantly.

**How to apply:** preserve this exception only in the welcome presentation; do not extend it to the Explore globe or other animations.
