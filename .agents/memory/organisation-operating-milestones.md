---
name: Organisation operating milestones
description: Product boundaries for organisation progression in Experiences.fun.
---

Organisation progression uses four operational milestones: profile ready, first experience, first verified delivery, and repeat participation. Keep organisation identity verification as a separate trust status. Do not add organisation XP, numeric ranks, public milestone badges, or unsupported impact claims.

**Why:** The product direction values verified real-world participation rather than gamified organisation status.

**How to apply:** Keep progression private and server-derived from the signed-in organisation’s profile, hosted experiences, and verified participant completions. Preserve these boundaries when extending organisation dashboards or progression.
