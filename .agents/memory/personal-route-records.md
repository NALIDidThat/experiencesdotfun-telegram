---
name: Personal route records
description: Product boundaries for participant-owned GPS activity records and elevation.
---

Participant-owned GPS activities are private personal records, separate from organisation-hosted Experiences. Do not award XP for recording them unless the user explicitly requests it. Phone GPS altitude is not a reliable elevation source; show elevation only when a trusted provider returns it, and leave it unavailable otherwise.

**Why:** the user asked for a personal route record without changing the hosted Experience model, and called out noisy phone altitude.

**How to apply:** Keep ownership checks and route points scoped to the participant. Do not connect route recording to XP awards. Never substitute GPS altitude or fabricated values for missing provider elevation.
