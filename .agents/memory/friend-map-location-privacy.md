---
name: Friend map location privacy
description: Privacy boundary for showing accepted friends on the Explore map.
---

Use an accepted friend’s existing profile city for an approximate city-centre marker only. Do not request or display live or precise device-location data.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to see friends’ approximate city locations without live or precise tracking.

**How to apply:** Preserve the accepted-friend boundary and city-level granularity in friend-map UI, location APIs, and related work.
