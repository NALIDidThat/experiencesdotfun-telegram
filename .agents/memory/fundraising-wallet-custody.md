---
name: Fundraising wallet custody
description: Product decision for charity SOL donations and on-chain totals.
---

Charity donations should go directly from the donor's wallet to the charity's wallet. Experiences.fun must not hold SOL or store private keys. Do not show raised totals or progress as confirmed until contributions have been verified on-chain.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose direct-to-charity-wallet donations rather than platform custody.

**How to apply:** Use donor-approved Solana Pay transfers for donation hand-off, explain that transfers are irreversible, and keep in-app totals unreported until transaction verification exists.
